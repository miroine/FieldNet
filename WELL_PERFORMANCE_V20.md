# FieldNet v20 — Well Performance & Artificial Lift

v20 is production-only; economics remains intentionally excluded.

## Models
- Nodal analysis: PI or Vogel IPR against selectable Beggs–Brill or homogeneous screening VLP.
- Completion: existing interval PI, skin and rate-dependent non-Darcy skin remain available.
- Gas lift: explicit injection-rate screening with diminishing-return pressure assistance and bounded optimization. It is not a mechanistic valve/string or compressor-network design model.
- ESP: generic parabolic head curve with affinity-law speed scaling, hydraulic power, recommended-rate envelope and optional NPSH check. Vendor curves are required for equipment selection.
- QA: nodal intersection, minimum-BHP and drawdown checks are reported separately from solver convergence.

Canonical project storage remains bar/m/Sm3/d and is displayed through the Norwegian SI or Field unit profile.
